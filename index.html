Doctoral studies in Civil Engineering

Construction Engineering and Management

I develop intelligent, human-centered construction systems that advance scientific knowledge and professional practice, with the goal of creating safer, healthier work environments for the construction workforce.

My work combines wearable and environmental sensing, occupational health and safety, workforce training, and data analytics. Much of my current research focuses on preventing heat stress and heat-related illness among construction workers through field monitoring, training, and evidence-based interventions.

Researcher

I study how sensing technologies, worker responses, and data-driven models can improve construction safety and worker health.

Educator

I teach through authentic construction problems, teamwork, and reflection, grounded in formal preparation in engineering education.

Engineer

I bring industry experience in construction project management and, currently, municipal infrastructure engineering.

Research program

Three interconnected research domains

Domain 01

Technology Innovation in Construction

Wearable sensing and IoT, building information modeling (BIM) and digital twins, and immersive technologies, examined both for their technical performance and for how workers adopt them.

Explore this domain: Technology Innovation in Construction →

Domain 02

Construction Safety and Health

Safety training and behavior, occupational risk assessment, and worker health, with an emphasis on field evidence and the evaluation of preventive interventions.

Explore this domain: Construction Safety and Health →

Domain 03

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Construction

Predictive and explainable models that turn multimodal construction data into interpretable information for workers, safety professionals, and project managers.

Explore this domain: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Construction →
