About

Researcher, engineering educator, and construction engineering professional.

Biography

I am a doctoral researcher in Civil Engineering at The University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA), concentrating in Construction Engineering and Management. I am a Graduate Research Assistant in the Safety Innovation and Intelligent Infrastructure (SI3) Lab and a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the School of Civil & Environmental Engineering, and Construction Management. My advisor is Dr. Ibukun Awolusi.

My dissertation, Proactive and Innovative Strategies for Heat Stress Prevention in Construction, examines how wearable sensing, environmental monitoring, training, and structured interventions can reduce heat-related risk among construction workers. In field studies with active construction workers, I synchronized physiological, environmental, and worker-reported data to characterize heat strain and to compare work–rest, hydration, and cooling interventions.

More broadly, my research develops intelligent, human-centered construction systems across three interconnected domains: technology innovation in construction, construction safety and health, and artificial intelligence and machine learning in construction. As a graduate research assistant, I have contributed to projects funded by the U.S. Department of Labor and the Transportation Infrastructure Precast Innovation Center (TRANS-IPIC).

I have taught and supported courses in construction safety, construction project management, building systems, sustainability, and structural engineering at UTSA, Michigan Technological University, Najafabad Higher Education Institute, and Amirkabir University of Technology. I completed UTSA’s Graduate Certificate in Engineering Education in 2025.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked in the construction industry in Iran from 2008 to 2021, in roles from civil engineer to senior project manager, on commercial, residential, and telecommunication infrastructure projects. I currently work as an Engineer III at Lockwood, Andrews & Newnam, Inc. (LAN) in San Antonio, on municipal infrastructure projects.
