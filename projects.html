Dissertation research

Proactive and Innovative Strategies for Heat Stress Prevention in Construction

Institution
The University of Texas at San Antonio · Safety Innovation and Intelligent Infrastructure (SI3) Lab
Advisor
Ibukun Awolusi, Ph.D.
Status
Doctoral studies in Civil Engineering

Field-based research that synchronizes physiological telemetry, environmental microclimate measurements, and worker-reported perceptions to characterize heat strain among construction workers, and evaluates structured work–rest, hydration, and cooling interventions under realistic working conditions. The research also evaluates heat-stress prevention training and develops prototype machine learning models for predicting physiological strain.
